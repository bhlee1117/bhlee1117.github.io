Research experience

  • In vivo dendritic voltage imaging (Harvard). High-speed voltage imaging across the dendritic trees of hippocampal CA1 pyramidal neurons in awake mice navigating virtual reality, to ask what dendritic excitations compute in vivo.
  • Real-time imaging of Arc mRNA synthesis in the live mouse brain (SNU). Used PP7 mRNA tagging to observe Arc transcription in live mice, identified transcription events in hundreds of neurons in hippocampal CA1 and retrosplenial cortex during memory formation and retrieval, and related them to Ca2+ activity in virtual reality.
  • Axonal β-actin mRNA dynamics (SNU). Visualized endogenous β-actin mRNA in live neurons from transgenic mice and developed the HybTrack tracking software to analyze how the mRNAs are transported and localized in axons.
